Account, margin and leverage

  • The spread is paid once (you buy at the ask and sell at the bid). Slippage is counted on a market entry and a stop exit; a limit order at the target has none.
  • Swap only counts when the holding is set to Overnight. Brokers charge triple swap on one weekday, usually Wednesday.
  • On USD-base pairs (USD/JPY…), profit is in the quote currency and converted to dollars at the entry price, so the dollar value is an approximation.

Building a position

One row per filled order, in the order they happened. Orders on the same side add to the position and move the average price; orders on the other side realize profit or loss against the average. Uses the instrument and costs from the Trade tab.

Price context

Shows where price sits against references you enter. It describes, it does not predict: price near the high does not mean it will fall, and near the low does not mean it will rise.

Scenarios

Every combination of stop, target and size, using the instrument, entry and costs from the Trade tab. Targets are multiples of the stop: 2 means a target twice as far as the stop.

Examples

A complete trade for each instrument, with every tab filled in. Prices are made up. Loading an example replaces what is on screen; Undo brings it back.
